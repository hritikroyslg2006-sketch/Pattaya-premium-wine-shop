Heritage & Craft

A Legacy Poured
Glass by Glass

Since 1987, L'Amour Du Vin has been Kolkata's most revered destination for the discerning wine enthusiast. Nestled in the heart of Park Street, our cellar holds over 400 labels carefully sourced from the storied vineyards of Bordeaux, Tuscany, Burgundy, and the Champagne region.

Every bottle we carry is personally verified by our resident Master Sommelier, ensuring that only wines of the highest provenance and aging potential find their place on our shelves. We believe wine is not merely a beverage — it is a narrative of soil, climate, and human artistry, bottled in time.

"A great wine does not merely please the palate — it speaks of the earth that raised it, the hands that tended it, and the years that transformed it into something extraordinary."
— Jean-Claude Moreau, Master Sommelier, L'Amour Du Vin
